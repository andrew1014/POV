# POV Footage Channel — Research Report (2026-09-04)

Deep-dive research on Andrew's idea: AI-generated POV (point-of-view) footage of
things you'd never normally see (e.g. "POV: a piglet running through a flower
field"), 15s or less, vertical for TikTok/Shorts. Cited sources at end.

## Verdict
**YES, launch a 15s animal/nature POV test channel — but as a LOW-COST EXPERIMENT
anchored on licensing/sponsorships, NOT platform ad payout.** Use free pixverse-v6
for the first 30-50 clips. Exit criteria: if 100k+ views or strong license/brand
interest within ~4-6 weeks, double down; else kill at near-zero cost.

## Has it been done? YES — but the precise AI-surreal-POV niche is early/under-served
- #POV on TikTok = 1+ TRILLION combined views (dominant format, mostly human drama skits).
- Animal POV (closest analog): mature, multiple 1M+ follower accounts (e.g. @fox.owl),
  real GoPro animal-POV clips routinely pull 10M-100M views.
- AI-generated POV: fast-growing but younger — some "AI nature shorts" YouTube channels
  crossed 500K-2M subs, but FEW are POV-first. So "AI surreal first-person micro-clip"
  is genuinely under-served right now.

## Why it hooks (cited)
- Curiosity-gap theory (Loewenstein 1994): an unresolved "never-seen" prompt drives
  sustained attention until resolved.
- First-3-second hook + completion rate = the dominant retention signal in vertical
  short-form (Nielsen/platform research).
- What works across viral POV: strong "you are here" hook in 1-2s, physical grounding
  (grass/water/fur/wind), a payoff beat, loop-friendly 6-15s.

## Monetization reality (the honest math)
- **TikTok Creativity Program: $0.30-$1.20 per 1k qualified views — BUT only for >60s
  original videos.** A pure 15s clip earns ~$0. ~1M views ≈ $300-$1,200 (long-form only).
- **YouTube Shorts: $0.05-$0.15 per 1k views** (≈$50-$150 per 1M views). Shorts <1min
  qualify but pay least.
- **UGC/stock licensing: the real upside** — Storyblocks/Shutterstock/Pond5 buy AI
  nature/animal stock clips; $50-$500/clip, no scale needed.
- **Brand deals/sponsorships: the revenue engine** — $100-$2,000+/post at 10K-100K
  engaged followers in a niche audience.
- **Net:** platform payout alone ≈ $50-150/1M Shorts views. The viable business =
  payouts + UGC licensing + sponsorships (the latter two dominate).

## Best starting niche
**Eye-level ANIMAL/NATURE POV in idyllic/surreal never-seen settings** (piglet in a
flower field, fox in a bioluminescent forest, whale in a kelp cathedral). Why:
1. Proven demand (GoPro/animal-POV virality).
2. Animals + flowing nature render BEST in current text-to-video (hide "AI slop" tells
   far better than faces/limbs/object physics).
3. Under-served among AI accounts (most do human drama or static images).
4. Strong licensing/brand appeal (toys, kids' apps, meditation apps, gaming).

AVOID: human-drama POV (saturated + AI faces render poorly), fantasy-humanoid
(saturated + hard), inanimate-object POV (novel but no proven audience).

## Risk factors (be honest)
1. **AI-content saturation** — algorithm buries low-retention AI clips.
2. **View-count volatility** — lottery-like; lumpy revenue.
3. **"Slop" perception** — audience/advertiser fatigue; uncanny motion kills shares.
4. **Platform labeling** — YouTube "altered/synthetic" disclosure + TikTok C2PA
   auto-labeling; labels can suppress reach, mandatory.
5. **Monetization eligibility** — 15s disqualifies the better TikTok program.
6. **Consistency/tool risk** — character/scene coherence across clips is hard.

## Cost plan (test cheap)
- First 30-50 clips on FREE pixverse-v6 (FAL) — near-zero cost.
- Reserve paid minimax-h3/hedra for higher-fidelity or character-consistency later.
- 15s clips for TikTok virality; ALSO make 60s+ compilation versions for YouTube
  Shorts payout eligibility.
