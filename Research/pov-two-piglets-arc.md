# POV Channel — "Two Piglets" Serialized Story Arc (draft 2026-09-04)

## Concept
A recurring POV series following a viewer-as-piglet in a flower field, with a
recurring COMPANION piglet for character consistency (the one-recurring-cast rule,
per the NOVA camera research). Each clip 15s, vertical, loopable. Free pixverse-v6.

## Arc (each scene = one 15s clip, ~4-part mini-series)
1. **Scene 1 — The Run (DONE, approved direction):** POV running through the
   sunlit wildflower meadow. Establishes setting + motion. (File:
   pov_piglet_flowerfield.mp4 / pov_piglet_butterfly.mp4)
2. **Scene 2 — The Companion + The Butterfly:** Still running; a SECOND piglet
   (companion) trots up beside you. You both notice a monarch butterfly passing —
   heads turn up together, curious. Establishes the recurring companion.
3. **Scene 3 — The Chase:** The companion piglet bounds ahead chasing the butterfly,
   ears flapping, you follow — energy + payoff beat.
4. **Scene 4 — The Landing (loopable close):** You catch up at the field edge; the
   butterfly lands on a flower nearby; both piglets pause, watching. Loop back to
   scene 1.

## Why this structure (from research)
- Recurring cast = emotional attachment + "same cameraman" consistency feel.
- Butterfly = curiosity-gap payoff (never-seen object of desire).
- Chase = motion energy (retention).
- Landing + loop = replay-friendly (algorithm signal).

## Consistency notes
- Keep the companion piglet visually similar each clip (pale pink, small, similar
  markings) so it reads as the SAME piglet across scenes (prompt: same descriptors).
- Keep the field + lighting consistent (sunlit meadow, wildflowers) across scenes.
- Scene 1 butterfly clip already exists as the butterfly payoff — can reuse/rework.
